# Privacy Policy

**Arbiter Sync**
Last updated: March 31, 2026

## Overview

Arbiter Sync is a local desktop application that syncs game assignments from Arbiter Sports to Google Calendar and sends confirmation emails to host schools. All data is stored locally on your own computer and is never transmitted to any third-party server.

## Data We Access

### Arbiter Sports
Arbiter Sync accesses your Arbiter Sports account to read your game schedule assignments. This is done through an automated browser session stored locally on your machine. No Arbiter credentials are stored by this application — your session is managed by the browser profile saved on your device.

### Google Calendar
Arbiter Sync requests access to your Google Calendar to create and update game events. Calendar data is written directly from your device to your own Google account and is never seen or stored by anyone else.

### Gmail
Arbiter Sync requests permission to send emails on your behalf. This is used solely to send day-before confirmation emails to host school contacts for your upcoming games. No emails are sent without your knowledge, and you can review and edit every email before it is sent.

## Data Storage

All data — including your game schedule, Google tokens, and email logs — is stored locally in the `data/` folder on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any external server.

## Data Sharing

We do not collect, share, sell, or transmit any of your personal data. This application runs entirely on your local machine.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses the following Google APIs:
- **Google Calendar API** — to create and update calendar events
- **Gmail API** — to send confirmation emails on your behalf

Use of these APIs is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
